01 Introduction

Welcome to Mindo: Daily Brain Training ("we", "our", or "us"), provided by Virajya Technologies (package name: com.virajyatechnologies.mindo). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent, safe, and enjoyable puzzle gaming experience.

This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you download, install, and play Mindo on Android devices. By using Mindo, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Summary: We do not collect your personal name, physical address, email, phone number, or contacts. Mindo uses an anonymous Player ID and local device storage for gameplay, scores, and cloud leaderboards.

02 Information We Collect and Use

When you play Mindo, the app collects only the minimum data required to deliver core gaming features and leaderboard rankings:

  • Player Display Identity (Non-Personal): The app generates an anonymous player identifier (e.g., #MND-4829) and allows you to choose an avatar mascot and display nickname. This identifier contains zero personally identifiable information (PII).
  • Gameplay & Score Data: Best scores, total accumulated puzzle XP, completed level progression, and daily streaks are stored locally on your device and synchronized to our dedicated cloud leaderboard.
  • Device Information & Diagnostics: Anonymous diagnostic metrics, app lifecycle events (e.g. app pause/resume for audio playback), and crash reports to ensure app stability and 60 FPS performance.

03 Third-Party Services & Advertising

Mindo integrates reputable third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device for ad delivery and cloud analytics:

  • Google AdMob: We use Google AdMob to display non-intrusive banner ads, milestone interstitial ads, and rewarded video ads. Google AdMob may use device advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID or IDFA) and coarse network state to serve relevant ads. You can learn more by visiting Google's Privacy & Terms.
  • Google Play Services: Used for core Android framework integration and app updates. View the Google Play Services Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore: Used solely to store anonymous player leaderboard rankings in an isolated database collection (mindo_game_players). No personal accounts or sensitive profiles are linked. View Firebase Privacy and Security.

04 Advertising & AdMob Policy Compliance

Mindo strictly complies with Google Play Developer Program Policies, Google AdMob Policies, and Better Ads Standards:

  • No Unexpected Full-Screen Ads: Interstitials are never shown unexpectedly during active gameplay or upon app launch. They only appear at natural milestone breaks (such as completing 5 puzzle levels).
  • Clear Ad Disclosures: In-game ad banners are clearly labeled with an "Ad" badge and positioned so they do not overlap gameplay or controls.
  • User-Initiated Rewarded Ads: Rewarded video ads are strictly optional and require explicit user consent to watch in exchange for game lifelines or continues.

05 Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Mindo is intended for a general audience (ages 13 and older). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

06 Account & Data Deletion (User Data Rights)

In compliance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion policies, Mindo gives you complete control over your game data and cloud records at all times.

Option 1: Instant In-App Data Deletion

1. Open the Mindo app on your mobile device.
2. Navigate to My Profile from the bottom navigation bar.
3. Scroll down to the Data & Privacy section.
4. Tap "Delete My Data & Reset Profile" and confirm.
This immediately purges your cloud record from Firebase Firestore (mindo_game_players) and resets all local scores and game progress.

Option 2: External Web / Email Request (Without App Installed)

If you have uninstalled the app or do not have access to your device, you can request permanent deletion of your leaderboard record by emailing our developer team directly:

  • Email: vinayurkude@gmail.com
  • Subject: Mindo Data Deletion Request
  • Details: Provide your Player ID (if known) or associated username/avatar. All matching cloud entries will be deleted within 48 hours.
