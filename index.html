Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our Sports Turf Booking application collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, and email address used for account creation.
  • Device & Usage Data: Device identifiers and FCM tokens used for push notifications and booking updates.
  • Payment Metadata: Transaction IDs and payment references processed securely via our payment gateway (Razorpay). We do not store raw card numbers or CVVs.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to facilitate slot bookings, send match notifications, manage team challenges, and process digital invoices.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates with trusted third-party providers:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, database, and push messaging.
  • Razorpay: Payment processing and transactions.

4. Data Deletion & Contact

You may request the deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time by contacting us at: support@yourturfapp.com.
