Scorer - Cricket Umpire

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Summary: Scorer - Cricket Umpire ("we", "our", or "app", package: com.vinayurkude.cricketscorer) provides cricket scoring, umpire tools, tournament management, player career statistics, and squad management. We respect your privacy and adhere to Google Play Developer Policies, GDPR, and CCPA standards. This policy explains exactly what data we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information to provide and improve the app experience:

  • Local Match & Scoring Data: Match scores, ball-by-ball events, custom team rosters, player profiles, tournament configurations, and umpire actions entered by you. Stored locally on your device.
  • Account & Profile Data (Cloud): When you sign in with Google, we store your name, email address, and profile information (player role, batting/bowling style, club name) in Firebase Firestore to enable career statistics tracking and cross-device sync.
  • Profile Photo: If you choose to set a profile picture, the image you select from your device gallery is uploaded and stored in your Firebase profile. Photo access (gallery/media) is used solely for this purpose and is never used for any other purpose.
  • Squad Invitation Data: When you use the "Add to My Squad" feature, we store squad connection requests (sender UID, recipient UID, status, and timestamp) in Firestore to manage the invitation lifecycle. This data is permanently deleted when either user deletes their account.
  • Device Contacts (Local Only): When you use the contact import tool to select teammates for a match squad, your device's address book is read locally on-device. Contact names, phone numbers, and email addresses are used only to populate your squad list. Contact data is never uploaded to any server, stored in any cloud database, or shared with any third party.
  • Device & Usage Data: Third-party analytics and ad networks may automatically receive non-personal technical information such as device model, OS version, approximate location (country/city level), and general app performance metrics.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Standard anonymous mobile identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / AAID) used by Google AdMob to serve relevant advertisements.

3. How We Use Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To record, calculate, and present real-time cricket scores, run rates, and player career statistics.
  • To maintain your player profile (name, role, photo) and synchronize career records across devices via Firebase.
  • To process squad invitations (sending, accepting, or declining "Add to My Squad" requests).
  • To persist your custom teams, match archives, and preferences (Dark Theme, Sunlight Mode).
  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdMob (supporting free access to the app).
  • To monitor app stability and maintain service reliability.

4. Device Permissions We Request

  • INTERNET: Required for Firebase, Google Sign-In, and AdMob network requests.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows checking network availability before initiating sync or ad requests.
  • VIBRATE: Used for tactile notification feedback on squad invitation events.

Note: Scorer does not request or require sensitive runtime permissions (such as Contacts or broad Storage access). Photo selection for match scorecards and profile avatars is handled via the secure, out-of-process Android System Photo Picker.

6. Data Storage & Security

  • Local-First Architecture: Scoring history, custom squads, and tournament data reside locally on your device.
  • Cloud Storage: Profile data, career statistics, and squad connections are stored in Google Firebase Firestore in the us-central1 region, secured with Firebase Security Rules.
  • Encryption in Transit: All network communication is secured via HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption.
  • No Data Sales: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

7. Account & Data Deletion Policy (Google Play Compliance)

In full compliance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion policies, you have complete control over your account and data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can initiate complete account and data deletion at any time within the app by going to Profile > Account & Data Deletion > Delete Account & All Data. This immediately and permanently deletes your Firebase authentication record, cloud directory profile, career statistics, squad connection requests, and all cloud-synced data.
  • Web / Remote Deletion Request: If you no longer have access to the app or device, you can request full account and data deletion by emailing vinay.urkude@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - Scorer" from your registered Google account email. All associated data will be purged within 48 hours.
  • Local Cache Clearing: You can delete all local device data via Android Settings > Apps > Scorer > Storage > Clear Storage, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Ad ID Reset: You can reset or opt out of personalized ads via Android Settings > Google > Ads > Reset Advertising ID / Delete Advertising ID.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Scorer - Cricket Umpire is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us immediately so we can remove such data.
